The Peiligang culture, about 7000 to 5000 BCE, held more than 100 sites in the Yi-Luo river basin in Henan, China. Its people farmed millet, raised pigs, fished with hemp nets and made some of China's earliest pottery.

Why it matters: It is one of the oldest farming cultures of the Yellow River region.

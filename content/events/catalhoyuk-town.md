Çatalhöyük was a large Neolithic settlement on the Konya Plain in southern Anatolia, occupied from about 7500 to 5600 BCE and at its height around 7000 BCE. It is known for its size and its apparently egalitarian social structure. The site lies in present-day Turkey.

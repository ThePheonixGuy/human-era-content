James Watt was a Scottish inventor and engineer who improved Thomas Newcomen's 1712 steam engine. He patented the separate condenser in 1769, and his engine entered commercial use in 1776.

Why it matters: Watt's engine was fundamental to the changes of the Industrial Revolution in Britain and the rest of the world.

The Wari Empire emerged around 600 CE in the Ayacucho Basin of Peru and lasted about 500 years. It linked provincial centres such as Cerro Baúl and Pikillacta to its capital Huari by roads.

Why it matters: Its roads and planned provincial cities were an Andean model that the Inca later built upon.

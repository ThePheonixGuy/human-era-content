Tell Brak in northern Syria began to urbanise in the late fifth millennium BCE. In its early Period F, about 3800 to 3600 BCE, it grew to around 130 hectares.

Why it matters: It shows that large cities in Upper Mesopotamia grew up alongside those of the south.

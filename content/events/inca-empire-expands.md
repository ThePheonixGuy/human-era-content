The Inca Empire, called the Realm of the Four Parts, was the largest empire in pre-Columbian America, centred on Cusco. The Inca civilisation rose in the Peruvian highlands in the early 13th century, and rapid expansion began around 1438. The Spanish arrived in 1532.

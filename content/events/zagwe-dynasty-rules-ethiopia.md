The Zagwe dynasty ruled large parts of the Ethiopian highlands from about 1137 to 1270. One tradition credits Mara Takla Haymanot with founding it in 1137.

Why it matters: King Gebre Meskel Lalibela appears in land grants dated 1204 and 1225.

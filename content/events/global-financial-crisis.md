The global financial crisis began in the United States in 2007, when securities tied to subprime mortgages lost their value and a liquidity crisis spread to banks around the world. It peaked with the bankruptcy of Lehman Brothers in September 2008.

Why it matters: It set off the Great Recession, the worst economic downturn since the Great Depression.

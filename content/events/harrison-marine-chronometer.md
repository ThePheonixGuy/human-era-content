The marine chronometer is a precision clock carried on ships to find longitude by comparing Greenwich time with local time. John Harrison's fourth model, H4, was tested at sea on voyages in 1761 and 1764 and kept excellent time. It solved the longitude problem that had caused many shipwrecks.

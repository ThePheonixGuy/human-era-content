Tiwanaku, in western Bolivia near Lake Titicaca, grew in prominence from about 375 CE to 700 CE, with its power in the Titicaca basin at a peak around 500 to 600 CE. The site features the terraced Akapana platform and the Gate of the Sun.

Why it matters: It was a major Andean ceremonial and political centre centuries before the Inca.

The Battle of Actium was a naval battle on 2 September 31 BCE in the Ionian Sea off Actium, Greece. Octavian's fleet, led by Marcus Agrippa, defeated the combined fleets of Mark Antony and Cleopatra. It was the climax of a rivalry between Octavian and Antony that had lasted over a decade.

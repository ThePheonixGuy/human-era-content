The Maykop culture of the western Caucasus flourished from roughly 3700 to 3000 BCE, from the Taman Peninsula to near the Dagestan border. Its burials, named for the royal Maykop kurgan in the Kuban valley, held more than 7,000 gold objects and about 1,000 silver items.

Why it matters: Its bronze and precious-metal work places the Caucasus among the earliest centres of metallurgy.

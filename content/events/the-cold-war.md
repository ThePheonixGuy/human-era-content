The Cold War was a geopolitical rivalry between the United States and the Soviet Union and their allies in the Western and Eastern blocs. It began after the Second World War and ended with the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991. It was called cold because the two superpowers never fought each other directly.

Why it matters: The rivalry drove an arms race, proxy wars and the Space Race.

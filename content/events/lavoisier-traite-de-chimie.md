Antoine Lavoisier was a French chemist central to the 18th-century chemical revolution. His 1789 textbook, the Traité élémentaire de chimie, set out a new system of chemistry based on careful measurement. It named oxygen, stated that mass is conserved in reactions and listed the chemical elements.

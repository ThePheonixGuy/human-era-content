Marie and Pierre Curie identified two new radioactive elements in 1898, announcing polonium in July and radium in December. Marie Curie won the 1903 Nobel Prize in Physics, shared with Pierre and Henri Becquerel, and the 1911 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. She remains the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.

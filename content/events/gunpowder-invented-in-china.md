Gunpowder, or black powder, is the earliest known chemical explosive, a mixture of sulfur, charcoal and potassium nitrate (saltpetre). Chinese alchemists developed it, and it was in use by about the 9th century CE. It became widely used in firearms, artillery and rocketry.

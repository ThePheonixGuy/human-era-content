Charlemagne, king of the Franks from 768 and of the Lombards from 774, was crowned emperor in 800 CE. He ruled most of Western and Central Europe and was the first recognised emperor in the West since the fall of the Western Roman Empire about three centuries earlier.

Why it matters: His Carolingian Empire shaped the political and cultural map of medieval Europe.

Mehrgarh, on the Kacchi Plain of Balochistan in Pakistan, is one of the earliest known sites in South Asia with evidence of farming and herding. Its people used wheat and barley and kept sheep, goats and cattle. Radiocarbon dating of teeth places its first period between 5250 and 4650 BCE, although an earlier estimate put the start near 7000 BCE, so the date is approximate.

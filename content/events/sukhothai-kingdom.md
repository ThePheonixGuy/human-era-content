The Sukhothai Kingdom was founded in 1238 by Si Inthrathit, a local chief who led a revolt against the governor at Sukhodaya. Under Ram Khamhaeng (1279 to 1298) it reached its peak and is credited with the Sukhothai script.

Why it matters: Thai tradition calls it the first Thai kingdom.

World War II was fought from 1939 to 1945 between the Allies and the Axis powers, and nearly every country in the world was drawn in. It was the deadliest conflict in history: some 60 to 75 million people died, most of them civilians.

Why it matters: The war ended with the first use of nuclear weapons and left the United States and the Soviet Union as rival superpowers.

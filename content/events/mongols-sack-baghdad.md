In early 1258 a large Mongol army under the prince Hulegu attacked Baghdad, capital of the Abbasid Caliphate, after a series of provocations from Caliph al-Musta'sim. Within weeks the city fell and was sacked. The caliph was killed together with hundreds of thousands of his subjects.

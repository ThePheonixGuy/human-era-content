After winning the Genpei War, Minamoto no Yoritomo set up the Kamakura shogunate in 1185, with 1192 the older date for his appointment as shōgun. Warrior government ran from Kamakura while the emperor stayed in Kyoto.

Why it matters: It began some seven centuries of warrior rule in Japan.

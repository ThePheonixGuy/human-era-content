Banpo, near Xi'an, was settled from about 4700 to 3600 BCE. Its excavation from 1954 to 1957 was the first large dig of a Yangshao site, and it revealed the first pottery kilns known in China.

Why it matters: It was the first large-scale dig of a Yangshao culture site.

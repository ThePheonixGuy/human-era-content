On 6 and 9 August 1945 the United States dropped atomic bombs on the Japanese cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki during the last days of World War II. The bombings killed between 150,000 and 246,000 people, most of them civilians.

Why it matters: They remain the only use of nuclear weapons in an armed conflict.

The Classic period of Maya civilisation began around 250 CE, with city-states such as Tikal building stepped pyramids. The Maya developed the most sophisticated writing system in the pre-Columbian Americas, as well as a detailed calendar and astronomy. The cities lived by trade and rivalry.

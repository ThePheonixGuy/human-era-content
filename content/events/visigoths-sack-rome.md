Alaric and the Visigoths entered Rome on 24 August 410 CE and plundered it for three days. Rome was no longer the imperial capital, but it was still the symbolic heart of the empire. It was the first time in almost 800 years that the city had fallen to a foreign enemy, and it shocked the Mediterranean world.

In the 13th and 14th centuries Mongol armies conquered much of Eurasia and built the Mongol Empire (1206 to 1368), the largest contiguous empire in history. At its height it reached from the Pacific to Central Europe, before it split into rival khanates from 1260.

Why it matters: The conquests killed millions, but the empire also tied East and West together through trade.

Holo's comment for each theme when its glowing trail is drawn. One line per theme, written as `theme-id: line`.

- agriculture: From the first crops around 501 HE (9500 BCE) to the Green Revolution of 11,950 HE (1950 CE), this trail follows how people learned to feed themselves.
- art: From the Great Pyramid around 7441 HE (2560 BCE) to the announcement of photography in 11,839 HE (1839 CE), this trail runs for about 4,400 years.
- disasters: Tambora erupted in 11,815 HE (1815 CE) and its ash cooled the planet so much that 11,816 HE (1816 CE) became the year without a summer.
- empires: The Roman Empire began in 9,974 HE (27 BCE) and its western half fell in 10,476 HE (476 CE), about five centuries later.
- environment: The warm, stable climate of the Holocene began around 301 HE (9700 BCE), and every event on this timeline happened inside it.
- exploration: Gagarin orbited Earth in 11,961 HE (1961 CE) and Apollo 11 reached the Moon in 11,969 HE (1969 CE), only about eight years apart.
- health: Smallpox, declared eradicated in 11,980 HE (1980 CE), was the first human disease to be wiped out completely.
- ideas: The Buddha, Confucius and Socrates all lived within roughly 165 years of one another.
- science: Copernicus in 11,543 HE (1543 CE), Galileo in 11,609 HE (1609 CE) and Newton's Principia in 11,687 HE (1687 CE) span just 144 years.
- tech: The Wright brothers flew in 11,903 HE (1903 CE), and only 66 years later Apollo 11 landed on the Moon.
- writing: Writing appeared around 6801 HE (3200 BCE), so the written record covers less than half of the Human Era.

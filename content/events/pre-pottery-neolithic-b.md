Pre-Pottery Neolithic B ran from about 8800 BCE to 6500 BCE and was centred on upper Mesopotamia and the Levant. Kathleen Kenyon defined it from her excavations at Jericho.

Why it matters: It carried farming villages across a wide region and set the stage for the first towns.

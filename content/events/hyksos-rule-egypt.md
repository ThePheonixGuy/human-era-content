The Fifteenth Dynasty of Hyksos kings ruled from Avaris in the Nile Delta from roughly 1650 to 1550 BCE. Ahmose I expelled them from Avaris during his reign, c. 1549 to 1524 BCE.

Why it matters: Their fall opened the New Kingdom, and their traditional credit for bringing the chariot and composite bow is disputed.

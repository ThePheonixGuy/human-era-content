The oldest evidence of foxtail millet cultivation comes from Cishan, along the ancient course of the Yellow River in China, carbon dated to around 8,000 years ago. The Cishan culture has been identified as the earliest to domesticate the crop, around 6500 to 5500 BCE. The date here is approximate.

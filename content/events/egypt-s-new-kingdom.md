The New Kingdom, also called the Egyptian Empire, covers ancient Egypt from the 16th to the 11th century BCE. It includes the Eighteenth, Nineteenth and Twentieth dynasties and followed the Second Intermediate Period. Radiocarbon dating places its start between 1570 and 1544 BCE.

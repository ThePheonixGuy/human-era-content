On 14 July 1789 revolutionary insurgents stormed the Bastille, a medieval fortress, armoury and prison in Paris. After four hours of fighting and 94 deaths, they entered the building.

Why it matters: The attack became a symbol of the French Revolution, which ended the absolute monarchy and spread ideas of citizenship and rights.

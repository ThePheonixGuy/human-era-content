Eridu, in what is now southern Iraq, was founded around 5400 BCE in the early Ubaid period on a virgin sand dune near the mouth of the Euphrates. Sumerian tradition counted it as the earliest city in southern Mesopotamia, and eighteen mudbrick temples were built one above another on the site. The date here is approximate.

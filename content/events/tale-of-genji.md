Murasaki Shikibu, a noblewoman and lady-in-waiting, wrote The Tale of Genji in the early 11th century. It is generally accepted that the tale was finished by 1021.

Why it matters: It is a classic of Japanese literature, often called the first novel, though that claim is disputed.

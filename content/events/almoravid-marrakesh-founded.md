The Almoravids founded Marrakesh around 1070, the date most modern historians use, with 1062 and 1078 also given. On 23 October 1086 they won the Battle of Sagrajas in Spain.

Why it matters: The dynasty joined Morocco and al-Andalus under one reforming movement.

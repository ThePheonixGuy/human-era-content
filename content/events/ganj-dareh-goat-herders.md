The settlement of Ganj Dareh in the Zagros mountains dates to about 8200 BCE and was abandoned about 7600 BCE. Its animal bones hold the earliest evidence for goat domestication in the world, with few adult males and many females and young males.

Why it matters: Managed goat herds gave early villages a steady supply of meat, milk and hides.

Chichén Itzá was a focal point of the northern Maya lowlands from about 600 to 1200 CE, and its apogee came between roughly 900 and 1050. Its step pyramid El Castillo honours the feathered-serpent deity Kukulcán.

Why it matters: El Castillo, a step pyramid about 30 metres high, still stands there.

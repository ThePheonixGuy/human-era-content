Sargon of Akkad founded the Akkadian Empire and is known for conquering the Sumerian city-states around 2300 BCE. He is sometimes called the first person in recorded history to rule an empire. His reign is traditionally dated 2334 to 2279 BCE, though a newer proposal gives 2316 to 2277 BCE.

Why it matters: His state set a pattern for later Mesopotamian empires.

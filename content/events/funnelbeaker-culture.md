The Funnelbeaker culture arose in north-central Europe between the lower Elbe and middle Vistula, from about 4100 to 2800 BCE. It brought farming to the pottery-using hunter-gatherers living north of its range.

Why it matters: It carried the farming way of life into northern Europe and left dolmen burials.

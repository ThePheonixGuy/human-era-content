The Green Revolution, or Third Agricultural Revolution, was a period of technology transfer that greatly raised crop yields. Its changes began in developed countries in the early 20th century and spread worldwide until the late 1980s. The high-yield wheat and rice of the 1950s and 1960s were central.

Why it matters: It helped feed a rapidly growing world population.

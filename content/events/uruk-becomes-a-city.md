Uruk, now the archaeological site of Warka in Iraq, was an ancient city in southern Mesopotamia on a now-dried channel of the Euphrates. It lies 93 kilometres northwest of Ur. The Uruk period named after it ran from about 4000 to 3100 BCE.

Why it matters: It is one of the first true cities, with temples, administration and specialised crafts.

DNA is a polymer of two polynucleotide chains coiled into a double helix that carries the genetic instructions of all known organisms. James Watson and Francis Crick published its structure in the journal Nature in April 1953.

Why it matters: The structure showed how genetic information is stored and copied, and it founded modern molecular biology.

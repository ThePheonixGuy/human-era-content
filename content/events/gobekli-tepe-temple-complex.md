Göbekli Tepe is a Neolithic site in southeastern Turkey, inhabited from about 9500 BCE to at least 8000 BCE during the Pre-Pottery Neolithic. It has large circular structures with stone pillars, among the world's oldest known megaliths, many decorated with carvings.

Why it matters: Its builders were hunter-gatherers, which suggests that large communal projects may have come before farming.

The Qing dynasty was a Manchu-led imperial dynasty of China that existed from 1636 or 1644 to 1912. It followed the Ming dynasty, whose capital Beijing fell in 1644. It was the last imperial dynasty in Chinese history and was succeeded by the Republic of China.

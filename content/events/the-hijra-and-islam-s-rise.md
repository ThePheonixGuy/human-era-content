The Hijrah was the journey of the Prophet Muhammad and his followers from Mecca to Medina. It took place in 622 CE and equates to 16 July 622 in the Julian calendar. Its year is the epoch of the Islamic Hijri calendars.

Why it matters: Muhammad founded the first Islamic state at Medina, and within a century Muslim armies had built an empire from Spain to Central Asia.

Joseph-Michel and Jacques-Étienne Montgolfier, paper makers from Annonay in France, invented the hot air balloon. On 21 November 1783 the first free flight by humans carried Pilâtre de Rozier and the Marquis d'Arlandes across Paris. It opened the age of human flight.

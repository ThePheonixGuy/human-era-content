Laozi is the name traditionally given to an ancient Chinese philosopher, also called Li Er or Lao Dan, regarded as the author of the Tao Te Ching, a foundational text of Taoism. Tradition places him in the 6th century BCE state of Chu as an archive keeper at the Zhou court. Whether he existed as described is debated.

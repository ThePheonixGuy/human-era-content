World War II began with the invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939, a joint attack by Nazi Germany, the Slovak Republic and the Soviet Union. It followed the Danzig crisis and the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact. Britain and France declared war on Germany two days later.

Why it matters: The war killed some 70 to 85 million people and reshaped the world order.

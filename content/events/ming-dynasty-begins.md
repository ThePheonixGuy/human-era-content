The Ming dynasty ruled China from 1368 to 1644 after the collapse of the Mongol-led Yuan dynasty. It was the last imperial dynasty ruled by the Han people. Beijing fell in 1644 to a rebellion led by Li Zicheng, though Ming remnant regimes persisted for some time.

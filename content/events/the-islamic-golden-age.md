The Islamic Golden Age was a period of cultural flourishing in the history of Islam, dated from the 8th century CE to the 14th century. It is understood to have begun under the Abbasid caliph Harun al-Rashid (786-809) with the House of Wisdom in Baghdad, which drew scholars from across the Muslim world to translate and study texts.

Why it matters: Scholars advanced medicine, astronomy and mathematics and preserved ancient texts that later reached Europe.

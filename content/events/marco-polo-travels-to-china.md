Marco Polo was a Venetian merchant and explorer who travelled through Asia along the Silk Road between 1271 and 1295. His Travels describe the wealth and size of the Mongol Empire and of China under the Yuan dynasty.

Why it matters: His book gave Europeans their first comprehensive look at China and the East.

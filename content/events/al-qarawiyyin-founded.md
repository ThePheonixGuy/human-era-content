Fatima al-Fihri founded the al-Qarawiyyin mosque in Fez in 857 to 859 according to tradition, though a foundation inscription suggests 877. It later became a centre of learning.

Why it matters: UNESCO and Guinness World Records cite it as the oldest continually operating higher learning institution in the world.

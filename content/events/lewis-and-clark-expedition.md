Captains Meriwether Lewis and William Clark led the Corps of Discovery west from near St. Louis in May 1804, to explore the territory gained in the Louisiana Purchase. They reached the Pacific coast in 1805 and returned in 1806. The expedition mapped the route and recorded many plants, animals and Native nations.

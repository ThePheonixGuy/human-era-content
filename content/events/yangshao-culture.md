The Yangshao was a Neolithic culture centred on the middle Yellow River in Henan, dated from about 5000 to 3000 BCE. It is named after the Yangshao site, first excavated in 1921 by Johan Gunnar Andersson.

Why it matters: It is the best known early farming culture of northern China.

The Yuan dynasty sent fleets against Japan in 1274 and 1281. Storms wrecked the first fleet, and a typhoon on 15 August 1281 devastated the second, which the Japanese called kamikaze.

Why it matters: The failed invasions are the origin of the term kamikaze, the divine wind.

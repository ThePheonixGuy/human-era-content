The Space Race was a Cold War contest between the United States and the Soviet Union to lead in spaceflight. It grew out of their missile rivalry after World War II, opened in 1955 with rival satellite plans and wound down with the joint Apollo-Soyuz mission in 1975.

Why it matters: It put the first satellite, the first person and the first Moon landing into history within twelve years.

The 1918-1920 flu pandemic was an exceptionally deadly global outbreak of the H1N1 subtype of influenza A, misleadingly called Spanish flu. The earliest probable cases were recorded in March 1918 in Kansas, with more in France, Germany and the United Kingdom in April. It infected roughly a third of the world's population.

Why it matters: It killed tens of millions of people and shaped later public health planning.

A group of independent Paris artists, including Monet, Renoir and Degas, held their first joint exhibition in April 1874. Their paintings stressed visible brush strokes, the changing qualities of light and ordinary subjects. A critic mocked Monet's Impression, Sunrise, and the name Impressionism stuck.

The Kunda culture was a group of Mesolithic hunter-gatherer communities in the Baltic forest zone, dated from about 8500 to 5000 BCE. It is named after the Estonian town of Kunda.

Why it matters: It shows people living in the northern forests as the ice sheets retreated.

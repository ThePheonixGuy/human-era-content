An Lushan marched out of Fanyang on 16 December 755 and the revolt lasted until 17 February 763, when the Yan emperor Shi Chaoyi died by suicide. Census totals fell from about 52.9 million in 755 to about 16.9 million in 764, though historians say the figures reflect broken administration as well as deaths.

Why it matters: It weakened the Tang for good and shifted power to regional military governors.

The Kedukan Bukit inscription, found near Palembang on Sumatra, is dated to 682 CE, with some sources giving 683. It is the earliest known inscription naming Srivijaya and places the kingdom's foundation on the Musi River.

Why it matters: Srivijaya went on to control sea trade through the Strait of Malacca.

The French Revolution was a decade of political and social upheaval in France, from the Estates General of 1789 to the Coup of 18 Brumaire in 1799. It ended the absolute monarchy and turned France into a republic.

Why it matters: Its ideas of citizenship, rights and popular sovereignty became founding principles of liberal democracy.

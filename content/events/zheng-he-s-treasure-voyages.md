Zheng He was a Chinese admiral and diplomat of the early Ming dynasty. Born Ma He into a Muslim family, he received the surname Zheng from the Yongle Emperor. His voyages along the Indian Ocean, the first beginning in 1405, greatly increased China's political and commercial influence.

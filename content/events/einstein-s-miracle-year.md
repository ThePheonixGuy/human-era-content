In 1905 Albert Einstein published four papers in the journal Annalen der Physik, known as the annus mirabilis papers. They covered the photoelectric effect, Brownian motion, special relativity and the equivalence of mass and energy.

Why it matters: The papers laid foundations of modern physics and changed the understanding of space, time, mass and energy.

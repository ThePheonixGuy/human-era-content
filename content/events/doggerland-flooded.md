Doggerland was a large area of land in the early Holocene that joined what is now Great Britain to mainland Europe. Rising seas inundated it between 10,000 and 7,000 years ago, and by around 6500 BCE Britain was cut off from the continent. Dogger Bank stayed an island until at least 5000 BCE. The date here is approximate.

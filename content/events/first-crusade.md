The First Crusade (1096-1099) was the first of the religious wars initiated, supported and at times directed by the Latin Church. Its aim was to return the Holy Land to Christian rule. The crusaders captured Jerusalem in 1099.

Why it matters: The Crusades created lasting contact, trade and conflict between Western Europe and the Islamic world.

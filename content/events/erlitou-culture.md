The Erlitou culture of western Henan and southern Shanxi dates to about 1750 to 1500 BCE by recent radiocarbon studies. Its namesake site had palace structures and bronze workshops, and it produced China's first large-scale bronzes, including the earliest known ding.

Why it matters: Its palaces and ritual bronzes foreshadow the Shang state that followed.

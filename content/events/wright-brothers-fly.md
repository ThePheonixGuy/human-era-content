Orville and Wilbur Wright were American aviation pioneers who built and flew the world's first successful airplane. They made the first controlled, sustained flight of an engine-powered, heavier-than-air aircraft with the Wright Flyer on 17 December 1903, near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, at what is now Kill Devil Hills.

Why it matters: Powered flight transformed travel, trade and warfare within decades.

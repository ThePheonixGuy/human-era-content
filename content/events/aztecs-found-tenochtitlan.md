The Mexica founded their capital Tenochtitlan on an island in Lake Texcoco, traditionally in 1325. The exact date is uncertain, and 13 March 1325 was chosen in 1925 to mark the city's 600th anniversary. It grew into one of the largest cities in the world and lies beneath modern Mexico City.

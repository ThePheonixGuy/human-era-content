The clay tablet Plimpton 322, written in cuneiform around 1800 BCE, lists number triples related to right triangles. Each row matches a Pythagorean triple, which shows that Babylonian scribes knew the relationship long before Pythagoras. Scholars still debate what the tablet was used for.

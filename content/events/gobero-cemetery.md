The Gobero site in the Ténéré desert of Niger, dated to around 8000 BCE, is the oldest known cemetery in the Sahara. It lay beside a freshwater lake and records how early people adapted to a changing environment. Gobero is the type site of the Kiffian and Tenerian cultures. The date here is approximate.

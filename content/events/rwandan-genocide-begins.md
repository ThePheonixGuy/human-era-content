Extremist Hutu forces began killing Tutsi and moderate Hutu people on 7 April 1994. Roughly 500,000 to 800,000 people were killed in about 100 days. The genocide ended in July when the Rwandan Patriotic Front took control. Diamond argues that land shortage and population pressure fed the killing alongside ethnic hatred.

Rapa Nui people carved the moai mostly between about 1250 and 1500 CE. More than 900 are known, most carved at the Rano Raraku quarry, and hundreds were moved to stone platforms around the coast.

Why it matters: The statues are the best-known work of Polynesian stone carving.

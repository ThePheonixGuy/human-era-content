Edward Jenner was an English physician who created the smallpox vaccine, the world's first vaccine. In 1796 he showed that cowpox gave protection against smallpox, and in 1798 he published his Inquiry into the Variolae vaccinae. The words vaccine and vaccination come from his term for cowpox.

Why it matters: It began the practice of vaccination, which eventually eradicated smallpox.

Genghis Khan was the founder and first khan of the Mongol Empire. After uniting the Mongol tribes, which was recognised at a gathering in 1206, he launched military campaigns that conquered large parts of China and Central Asia.

Why it matters: The empire his successors built became the largest contiguous land empire in history and linked East and West.

The eruption of Thera (Santorini) around 1600 BCE was rated VEI 7, though archaeological evidence suggests a date near 1550 to 1480 BCE. It destroyed the Minoan settlement at Akrotiri, and earthquakes and tsunamis struck Cretan sites such as Petras and Palaikastro.

Why it matters: It was one of the largest volcanic events in human history, and the Minoans recovered to reach what is considered the zenith of their culture.

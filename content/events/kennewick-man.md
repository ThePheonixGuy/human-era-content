Kennewick Man lived about 8,400 to 8,690 years ago, based on calibrated radiocarbon dating, and was found in 1996 on a Columbia River bank in Kennewick, Washington. A 2015 genome study linked him most closely to modern Native Americans.

Why it matters: His genome helped answer who the early peoples of the Pacific Northwest were.

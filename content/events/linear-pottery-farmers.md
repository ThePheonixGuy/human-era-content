The Linear Pottery culture flourished in central Europe from about 5500 to 4500 BCE. It began on the middle Danube and spread about 1,500 kilometres along the rivers in roughly 360 years, a major step in the spread of agriculture across Europe. Its name comes from the lines incised on its simple cups, bowls and vases.

In April 1815 Mount Tambora on Sumbawa in present-day Indonesia erupted in what is regarded as the most powerful volcanic eruption in recorded human history. It had a volcanic explosivity index of 7 and ejected 37 to 45 cubic kilometres of material. It is the most recent confirmed VEI-7 eruption.

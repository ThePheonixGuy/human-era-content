The Age of Discovery, or Age of Exploration, was the period from about the 15th to the 17th century when European seafarers explored, colonised and conquered regions across the globe. It overlapped with the Age of Sail and connected previously isolated parts of the world.

Why it matters: It created the first worldwide network of trade and also of conquest and disease.

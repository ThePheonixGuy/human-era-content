The Yuan dynasty was a Mongol-led imperial dynasty of China and a successor state to the Mongol Empire. Kublai, fifth khagan-emperor of the Mongol Empire, founded it, and it lasted from 1271 to 1368 CE. It followed the Song dynasty and preceded the Ming.

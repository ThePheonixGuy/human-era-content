The Third Punic War was the last of the wars between Carthage and Rome and was fought entirely in Carthaginian territory in what is now northern Tunisia. After the Second Punic War ended in 201 BCE, the peace treaty barred Carthage from waging war without Rome's permission. Rome destroyed the city in 146 BCE.

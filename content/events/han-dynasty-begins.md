The Han dynasty was founded by Liu Bang in 202 BCE, after the short-lived Qin dynasty and a struggle with the Chu known as the Chu-Han Contention. It lasted until 220 CE and was interrupted briefly by Wang Mang's Xin dynasty. Historians divide it into the Western Han and the Eastern Han.

Why it matters: Han rule set the pattern for imperial China, and the Han Chinese people take their name from it.

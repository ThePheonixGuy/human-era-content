The cave art of Cueva de las Manos in Patagonia is dated to between about 7300 BCE and 700 CE. Its hand stencils number 829 left hands against 31 right hands, which suggests the painters held the bone spray pipe in their right hand.

Why it matters: The cave is a long record of Patagonian art made by many generations.

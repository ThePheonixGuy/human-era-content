The Naqada culture was a Predynastic culture of Upper Egypt, named for the town of Naqada and dated to about 4000 to 3000 BCE. Archaeologists divide it into Naqada I, II and III, and a 2013 radiocarbon study placed its beginning between 3800 and 3700 BCE. The date here is approximate.

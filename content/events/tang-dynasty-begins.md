The Tang dynasty ruled China from 618 to 907, with an interregnum from 690 to 705. It followed the Sui dynasty and preceded the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. Historians generally see it as a high point of Chinese civilisation and a golden age of cosmopolitan culture.

Why it matters: Its poetry, trade along the Silk Road and capital Chang'an shaped East Asia for centuries.

The volcanic island of Krakatoa erupted from 19 May to 21 October 1883. The main eruption on 27 August destroyed over 70 percent of the island and its surroundings, which collapsed into a caldera. It had a volcanic explosivity index of 6, and tsunamis it caused killed tens of thousands.

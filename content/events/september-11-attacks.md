The September 11 attacks of 2001 were a coordinated series of suicide attacks by the Islamic terrorist group al-Qaeda against the United States. Nineteen hijackers took over four airliners. Two were flown into the Twin Towers of the World Trade Center in New York and a third into the Pentagon, and the fourth crashed in Pennsylvania. Nearly 3,000 people died.

Why it matters: The attacks led to the US-led war on terror and the wars in Afghanistan and Iraq.

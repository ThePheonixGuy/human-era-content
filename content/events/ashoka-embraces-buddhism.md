After the bloody conquest of Kalinga around 261 BCE, the Maurya emperor Ashoka turned to Buddhist ethics. He inscribed edicts promoting tolerance and moral conduct on rocks and pillars across his empire. He also sent missions to spread Buddhism across Asia.

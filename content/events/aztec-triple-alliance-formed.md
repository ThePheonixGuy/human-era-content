Tenochtitlan, Tetzcoco and Tlacopan formed the Triple Alliance in 1428, after the Tepanec War of 1427 to 1428 against Azcapotzalco. The article also dates the formal treaty to 1430.

Why it matters: Tenochtitlan was one of its three members.

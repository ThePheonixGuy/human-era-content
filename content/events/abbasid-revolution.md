Abu Muslim began an open revolt at Merv in 747, and al-Saffah was recognised as caliph at Kufa in November 749. The Abbasids destroyed the army of Marwan II at the Battle of the Zab on 16 January 750.

Why it matters: Al-Saffah, a descendant of Muhammad's uncle al-Abbas, became the first Abbasid caliph.

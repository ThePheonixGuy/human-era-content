Sumer, in southern Mesopotamia, is the earliest known civilisation. It emerged between the 5th and 4th millennia BCE and is one of the cradles of civilisation. Its people lived along the Tigris and Euphrates valleys in city-states such as Ur, Lagash and Kish.

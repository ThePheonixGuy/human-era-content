Cheddar Man died around 8300 BCE, aged roughly 18 to 23, and was found in Gough's Cave in Cheddar Gorge, England, in December 1903. He is the oldest nearly complete human skeleton in Great Britain.

Why it matters: His DNA gives a direct look at the people who lived in Britain after the ice retreated.

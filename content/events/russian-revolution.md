The October Revolution was the second of two revolutions in Russia in 1917 and began as an insurrection in Petrograd on 7 November 1917 (25 October in the old calendar). Vladimir Lenin's Bolsheviks led it. It led to the Russian Civil War and later the founding of the Soviet Union.

Why it matters: It created the first state led by a communist party and set up decades of rivalry between ideological blocs.

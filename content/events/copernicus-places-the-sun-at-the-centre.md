Nicolaus Copernicus's book De revolutionibus orbium coelestium was first printed in Nuremberg in 1543. It proposed a heliocentric model of the universe as an alternative to Ptolemy's geocentric system, which had been accepted since antiquity.

Why it matters: The book is usually seen as the start of the Scientific Revolution.

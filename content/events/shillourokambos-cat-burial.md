At Shillourokambos in Cyprus, a grave dug in 2004 held a person and a cat buried side by side, estimated to be about 9,500 years old. It is the oldest evidence of cat domestication.

Why it matters: It pushed the story of the house cat back thousands of years before the Egyptian images that were once thought the earliest.

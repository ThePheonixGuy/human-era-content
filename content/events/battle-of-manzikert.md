On 26 August 1071 the Seljuk leader Alp Arslan defeated the Byzantine army near Manzikert and captured Emperor Romanos IV Diogenes. Byzantine control of Anatolia weakened and Turkic settlement followed.

Why it matters: It opened Anatolia to Turkic rule.

Stonehenge, on Salisbury Plain in Wiltshire, England, was built in phases from about 3100 to about 1600 BCE. Its earliest phase was a circular earth bank and ditch, and the famous circle of large sarsen stones was raised between 2600 and 2400 BCE. The date here is approximate.

The Botai culture of northern Kazakhstan dates to about 3700 to 3100 BCE and left at least 153 pit-houses at its namesake settlement. Horse bones, corral-like enclosures and dung link it to the earliest evidence of horse husbandry.

Why it matters: It is a key site in the debate over when people first kept horses.

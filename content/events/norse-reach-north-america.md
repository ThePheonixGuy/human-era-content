Leif Erikson and Norse sailors landed in North America around 1000 CE and set up a settlement the sagas call Vinland. The remains of a Norse settlement at L'Anse aux Meadows in Newfoundland have been dated to about 1021. It is the earliest known European presence in the Americas.

Ō no Yasumaro presented the Kojiki to Empress Genmei in the first month of 712, after she ordered it in 711. He wrote it in Classical Chinese from what the court attendant Hieda no Are had memorised.

Why it matters: It is the oldest surviving book of Japanese myths and early history.

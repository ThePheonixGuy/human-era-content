Eleven rock-hewn churches at Lalibela are traditionally credited to the Zagwe king Gebre Meskel Lalibela, who ruled about 1181 to 1221, though the dating is not firmly established. UNESCO inscribed them in 1978.

Why it matters: They are carved from living rock in the Ethiopian highlands.

The Spirit Cave mummy is about 11,500 years old, a date set in 1996 that replaced an earlier guess of 1,500 to 2,000 years. Sydney and Georgia Wheeler found it in Nevada in 1940. A 2018 DNA study found it closely related to Indigenous peoples of the Americas, and the remains went back to the Fallon Paiute-Shoshone Tribe in 2016.

Why it matters: It is among the oldest mummified human remains in North America and links early burial to living communities.

Early alphabetic scripts used a few dozen signs for consonants instead of thousands of word signs. Alphabets appear in Canaanite and Ugaritic inscriptions in the second millennium BCE. The Phoenician script of the first millennium BCE spread widely and gave rise to the Greek and Latin letters.

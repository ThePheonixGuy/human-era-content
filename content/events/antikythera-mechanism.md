A geared bronze device built by Greek craftsmen in the second century BCE modelled the motions of the Sun and Moon and could predict eclipses. It was found in a shipwreck near the island of Antikythera and identified in 1901. It is the oldest known analogue computer, and nothing as complex appears again for over a thousand years.

The Wall Street crash of 1929 was a major US stock market crash that began in October 1929 with a sharp fall in prices on the New York Stock Exchange. Prices collapsed on 29 October, known as Black Tuesday. It shattered confidence in the US banking system and led into the worldwide Great Depression.

Why it matters: The Depression brought mass unemployment worldwide and reshaped economic policy.

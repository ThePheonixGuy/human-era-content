Enheduanna, high priestess of the moon god Nanna at Ur and daughter of Sargon of Akkad, composed hymns that survive on clay tablets. She wrote in Sumerian and is the earliest author known by name. Her poems to the goddess Inanna influenced later religious writing in Mesopotamia.

The House of Wisdom, or Grand Library of Baghdad, was a major Abbasid academy and intellectual centre in Baghdad. Scholars there worked during the Islamic Golden Age, including al-Khwarizmi, whose book on calculation gave algebra its name. Its precise origins under Harun al-Rashid or al-Ma'mun are debated.

Why it matters: Scholars there preserved and extended Greek, Persian and Indian knowledge, which later reached Europe.

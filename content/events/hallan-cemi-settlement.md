Hallan Çemi was founded around 9500 BCE in eastern Anatolia. About 60 per cent of its chipped stone came from obsidian sourced roughly 100 km away, and pigs there are among the earliest animals domesticated in the region.

Why it matters: It is an early example of long-distance trade and animal keeping among people who were not yet full farmers.

On 1 June 907 the warlord Zhu Wen deposed the last Tang emperor, Emperor Ai, and founded the Later Liang. This began the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period.

Why it matters: It closed a dynasty that had ruled for almost three centuries.

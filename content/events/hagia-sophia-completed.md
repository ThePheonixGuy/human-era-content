Hagia Sophia in Istanbul was completed in 537 CE as the last of three church buildings on the site raised by the Eastern Roman Empire. It became the world's largest interior space and among the first to use a fully pendentive dome. It was a church until 1453, then a mosque, a museum from 1935 and a mosque again from 2020.

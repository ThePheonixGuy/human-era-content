Polynesians first settled New Zealand in the mid-13th to mid-14th century, with the main settlement about 1320 to 1350. No human trace has been confidently dated before the Kaharoa Tephra, a volcanic layer from about 1314.

Why it matters: It was the last large habitable landmass settled by people.

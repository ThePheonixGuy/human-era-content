People occupied Çayönü near Diyarbakır in southeastern Turkey from about 8630 BCE to 6800 BCE, during the Pre-Pottery Neolithic B. Excavations from 1964 to 1991 made it a key site for early farming life, and it may be where pigs were first domesticated.

Why it matters: It shows a village economy forming as hunting gave way to herding and farming.

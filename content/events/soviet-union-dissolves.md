On 26 December 1991 the Soviet Union was formally dissolved and ceased to exist as a sovereign state. It also ended the federal government and Mikhail Gorbachev's efforts to reform the Soviet political and economic system.

Why it matters: The dissolution ended the Cold War and left fifteen independent successor states.

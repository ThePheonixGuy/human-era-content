Sannai-Maruyama in northern Japan was occupied from about 3900 to 2200 BCE and is the largest Jōmon settlement found so far, with 648 pit dwellings. Hunter-gatherers first used it temporarily, and the final shift to a sedentary way of life came around 2900 BCE.

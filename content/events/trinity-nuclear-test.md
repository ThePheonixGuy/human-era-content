Trinity was the first detonation of a nuclear weapon, carried out by the US Army at 5:29 a.m. on 16 July 1945 as part of the Manhattan Project. It tested an implosion-design plutonium bomb, the same design as the Fat Man bomb dropped on Nagasaki on 9 August 1945.

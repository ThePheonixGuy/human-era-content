The Declaration of Independence is the founding document of the United States. The Second Continental Congress adopted it unanimously on 4 July 1776 at the Pennsylvania State House, later renamed Independence Hall, in Philadelphia.

Why it matters: It declared the thirteen colonies independent from Britain and stated that people hold equal rights, inspiring movements around the world.

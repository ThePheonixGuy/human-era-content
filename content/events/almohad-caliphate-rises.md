The Almohad movement began under Ibn Tumart, recognised as the Mahdi around 1121. After his death in 1130 Abd al-Mu'min founded the caliphate, and he captured Marrakesh in 1147 after an eleven-month siege.

Why it matters: Abd al-Mu'min founded the caliphate after Ibn Tumart died in 1130.

The Berlin Wall was a guarded concrete barrier around West Berlin that East Germany began building on 13 August 1961. It divided the city until the East German border opened in November 1989, after which crowds began taking the wall apart. Germany reunified in October 1990.

Why it matters: The fall of the wall became the symbol of the end of the Cold War division of Europe.

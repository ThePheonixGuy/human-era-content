Jarmo in northern Iraq was occupied from about 7090 to 4950 BCE. Its roughly 150 residents lived in about 25 houses, grew wheat and lentils and kept goats, sheep and dogs.

Why it matters: Its later levels are among the oldest in which pottery has been found.

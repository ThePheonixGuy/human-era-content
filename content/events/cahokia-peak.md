The city of Cahokia existed from about 1050 to 1350 CE and peaked around 1100 CE, with an estimated population of 15,000 to 20,000. Its Monks Mound is the largest earthen structure in the Americas north of Mexico.

Why it matters: It was the largest city north of Mexico for its time.

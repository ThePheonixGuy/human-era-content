In December 1938 the German chemists Otto Hahn and Fritz Strassmann found that uranium nuclei split when bombarded with neutrons. Lise Meitner and Otto Frisch explained the process as nuclear fission. The reaction releases an enormous amount of energy, which led to nuclear reactors and weapons.

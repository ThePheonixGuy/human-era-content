The Internet protocol suite, commonly called TCP/IP, is a framework for the communication protocols of the Internet. Its foundation is the Transmission Control Protocol and the Internet Protocol. ARPANET switched to TCP/IP on 1 January 1983.

Why it matters: A shared protocol let separate networks join into one internet.

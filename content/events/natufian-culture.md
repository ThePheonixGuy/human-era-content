The Natufian culture of the late Epipalaeolithic Near East supported sedentary or semi-sedentary communities even before farming began. Natufians exploited wild cereals and hunted mountain gazelles, and some evidence points to deliberate cultivation of rye at Tell Abu Hureyra. The culture began well before the Human Era, so this entry marks its closing phase. The date here is approximate.

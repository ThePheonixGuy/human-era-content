The elite of Mapungubwe moved to Mapungubwe Hill around 1220 CE, at the Shashe and Limpopo confluence in South Africa. The kingdom exported gold and ivory via Sofala, and the hill was abandoned about 1300. The golden rhinoceros was found in 1933.

Why it matters: It was a leading trading state in southern Africa.

The early Muslim conquests were a series of wars begun by the Prophet Muhammad and continued by the Rashidun and Umayyad caliphates. Muslim armies expanded rapidly out of Arabia after Muhammad founded the first Islamic state in Medina in 622. Muslim rule spread across the Middle East, North Africa and beyond.

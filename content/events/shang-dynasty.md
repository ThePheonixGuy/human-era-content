The Shang dynasty, also called the Yin dynasty, ruled in the Yellow River valley during the 2nd millennium BCE. It traditionally succeeded the Xia dynasty and was followed by the Western Zhou. Modern scholarship dates it between the 16th and 11th centuries BCE.

The brothers Tughril and Chaghri founded the Seljuk Empire in 1037. The Seljuks defeated Masʽud I at the Battle of Dandanaqan in 1040, and Tughril entered Baghdad in 1055.

Why it matters: Tughril's entry into Baghdad in 1055 ended Buyid influence there.

Sputnik 1 was the first artificial Earth satellite. The Soviet Union launched it into low Earth orbit on 4 October 1957. It sent a radio signal for three weeks until its batteries ran out and re-entered the atmosphere on 4 January 1958.

Why it matters: It began the Space Race and the space age.

Ötzi, Europe's oldest known natural human mummy, lived in the 4th millennium BCE and is dated to about 3275 to 3230 BCE. Hikers found him on 19 September 1991 in the Ötztal Alps, just inside Italy near the border with Austria. An arrowhead in his left shoulder and other wounds lead researchers to believe he was killed by another person. The year shown is the end of that range and is approximate.

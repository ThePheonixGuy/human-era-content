Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land on the Moon, in July 1969. Commander Neil Armstrong and lunar module pilot Buzz Aldrin landed while command module pilot Michael Collins orbited above. Armstrong was the first person to step onto the lunar surface.

Why it matters: The landing met the goal set by President Kennedy and remains the high point of human spaceflight.

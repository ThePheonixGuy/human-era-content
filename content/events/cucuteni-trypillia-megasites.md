The Cucuteni-Trypillia culture lived in southeast Europe from the Carpathians to the Dnieper. In its middle phase, about 4100 to 3500 BCE, it built megasites with up to 3,000 structures and possibly 20,000 to 46,000 residents.

Why it matters: They count among the largest settlements of their time and are sometimes called early forms of cities.

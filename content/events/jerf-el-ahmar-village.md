The village of Jerf el Ahmar in Syria was occupied from about 9500 BCE to 8700 BCE. Its round and rectangular buildings later disappeared under the lake behind the Tishrin Dam, and it yielded the earliest evidence of lentil domestication in the region.

Why it matters: It shows how people were experimenting with crops and communal buildings well before pottery.

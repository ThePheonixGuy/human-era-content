The Space Shuttle Challenger broke apart 73 seconds after launch on 28 January 1986, killing all seven crew. The cause was the failure of an O-ring seal in a solid rocket booster in cold weather. It was the first fatal accident in flight for an American spacecraft with a crew aboard.

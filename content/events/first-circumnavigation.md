The Magellan-Elcano expedition was a Spanish expedition planned and first led by the Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan. It left Spain in 1519 to find a sea route to the Spice Islands and was completed by Juan Sebastian Elcano, whose ship returned in 1522. It made the first circumnavigation of the Earth.

Why it matters: It showed that the world's oceans are connected and gave a measure of the planet's size.

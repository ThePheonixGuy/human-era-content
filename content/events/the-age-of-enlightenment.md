The Age of Enlightenment, or Age of Reason, was a period of intellectual and cultural flourishing in Europe that emerged in the late 17th century. It peaked in the 18th century, when its ideas spread across Europe and into the colonies in the Americas and Oceania.

Why it matters: Its emphasis on reason, science and individual rights inspired revolutions and modern liberal democracy.

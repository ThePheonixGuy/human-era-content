A magnitude 9.1 to 9.3 earthquake off Sumatra on 26 December 2004 produced tsunamis that killed over 200,000 people in fourteen countries. The undersea rupture was along the boundary of the Burma and Indian plates. It was one of the deadliest natural disasters on record.

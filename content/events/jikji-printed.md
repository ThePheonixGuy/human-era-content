The Jikji, a Korean Buddhist book, was printed with metal type at Heungdeok Temple in Cheongju in July 1377. It is called the world's oldest extant book printed with movable metal type.

Why it matters: UNESCO recognised it on the Memory of the World Register in September 2001.

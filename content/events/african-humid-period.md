During the African humid period North Africa was wetter than it is today, and grasses, trees and lakes covered much of the Sahara. The period began about 14,600 years ago, before the Human Era, and ended between 6,000 and 5,000 years ago. People settled the region, domesticated cattle, goats and sheep, and left rock paintings and canoes. The end date is approximate.

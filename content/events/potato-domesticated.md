Farmers in southern Peru and northwestern Bolivia, around Lake Titicaca, domesticated the potato from wild Solanum species about 7,000 to 10,000 years ago. Of more than 55 wild diploid potato species, only one lineage was selected by people for domestication. The year shown is the middle of that range and is approximate.

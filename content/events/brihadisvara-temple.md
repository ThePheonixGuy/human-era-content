Chola emperor Rajaraja I built the Brihadisvara Temple at Thanjavur between 1003 and 1010 CE. UNESCO inscribed it in 1987 as part of the Great Living Chola Temples.

Why it matters: It stands as the high point of Chola temple architecture.

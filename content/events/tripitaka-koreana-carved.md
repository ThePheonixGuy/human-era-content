The first set of the Tripitaka Koreana woodblocks was carved from 1011 to 1087. The second set, usually meant by the name, was begun in 1237 and took 12 years, with 81,352 blocks now kept at Haeinsa temple.

Why it matters: The blocks have been kept at Haeinsa since 1398.

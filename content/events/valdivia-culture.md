The Valdivia culture thrived along the coast of the Santa Elena peninsula in Ecuador between 3500 and 1500 BCE. Its pottery began rough and practical and grew more delicate and larger over time. Its earliest pottery was long thought to be the oldest made by a coastal culture in South America.

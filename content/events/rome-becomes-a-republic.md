The Roman Republic was the era of classical Roman civilisation that began with the overthrow of the Roman Kingdom, traditionally in 509 BCE. It ended in 27 BCE with the establishment of the Roman Empire after the War of Actium. During this time Rome expanded from its immediate surroundings to dominate the whole Mediterranean.

Why it matters: Its offices, Senate and legal ideas influenced later republics.

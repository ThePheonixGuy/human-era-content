The Renaissance was a European cultural movement at the end of the Late Middle Ages and the start of the early modern era. It is defined as covering the 15th and 16th centuries or, more broadly, the 14th to the 17th century. It revived the literary, philosophical and artistic achievements of classical antiquity.

Why it matters: Its art and learning spread across Europe and changed painting, science and thought.

The Badarian culture flourished between about 4400 and 4000 BCE in Upper Egypt, and may have begun by 5000 BCE. It gives the earliest direct evidence of agriculture in Upper Egypt.

Why it matters: It is an early step in the Predynastic period that led to the unification of Egypt.

Lepenski Vir lies on the right bank of the Danube in the Iron Gates gorge of eastern Serbia. In its late phase, from about 6300 to 6000 BCE, people built trapezoidal houses and carved monumental sculpture, including fish figures with human-like faces. The date here is approximate.

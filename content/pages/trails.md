Holo's comment for each theme when its glowing trail is drawn. One line per theme, written as `theme-id: line`.

- agriculture: From the first crops around 9500 BCE to the Green Revolution of 1950, this trail follows how people learned to feed themselves.
- art: From the Great Pyramid around 2560 BCE to the announcement of photography in 1839, this trail runs for about 4,400 years.
- disasters: Tambora erupted in 1815 and its ash cooled the planet so much that 1816 became the year without a summer.
- empires: The Roman Empire began in 27 BCE and its western half fell in 476 CE, about five centuries later.
- environment: The warm, stable climate of the Holocene began around 9700 BCE, and every event on this timeline happened inside it.
- exploration: Gagarin orbited Earth in 1961 and Apollo 11 reached the Moon in 1969, only about eight years apart.
- health: Smallpox, declared eradicated in 1980, was the first human disease to be wiped out completely.
- ideas: The Buddha, Confucius and Socrates all lived within roughly 165 years of one another.
- science: Copernicus in 1543, Galileo in 1609 and Newton's Principia in 1687 span just 144 years.
- tech: The Wright brothers flew in 1903, and only 66 years later Apollo 11 landed on the Moon.
- writing: Writing appeared around 3200 BCE, so the written record covers less than half of the Human Era.

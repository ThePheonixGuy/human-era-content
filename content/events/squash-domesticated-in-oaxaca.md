Guilá Naquitz Cave in Oaxaca, Mexico, holds the earliest known evidence of squash (Cucurbita) domestication, dated between about 8,000 and 10,750 years before present. That is roughly 4,000 years before maize and beans were domesticated in the region. The cave also yielded teosinte, the ancestor of maize, and bottle gourds. The date here is approximate.

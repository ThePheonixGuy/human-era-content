Jiahu, a moated Neolithic village in central China, was settled around 7000 BCE. Its burials held flutes carved from the wing bones of red-crowned cranes, believed to be among the oldest playable musical instruments in the world. The site also shows evidence of alcohol made by fermenting rice, honey and hawthorn leaves. The date here is approximate.

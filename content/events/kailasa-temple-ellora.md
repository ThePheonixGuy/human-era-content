The Kailasa Temple at Ellora is generally attributed to the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I, who reigned from about 756 to 773. Carvers cut it from a single rock face, working from the top downward.

Why it matters: The temple was carved from a single rock face rather than built from blocks.

Egyptian hieroglyphs were the formal writing system of ancient Egypt, combining ideographic, logographic, syllabic and alphabetic elements with more than 1,000 distinct characters. Cursive hieroglyphs were used for religious texts on papyrus and wood. The later hieratic and demotic scripts derived from them.

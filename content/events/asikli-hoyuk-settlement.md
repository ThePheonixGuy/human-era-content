Aşıklı Höyük in central Anatolia was occupied from about 8200 to 7400 BCE. It is one of the earliest aceramic Neolithic sites on the plateau, and its nearby obsidian reached places as far as Cyprus and Iraq.

Why it matters: It is evidence of early settled life and long-range exchange in Anatolia.

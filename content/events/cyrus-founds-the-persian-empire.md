Cyrus II of Persia, known as Cyrus the Great, founded the Achaemenid Empire around 550 BCE by defeating the Median Empire. He expanded across much of West and Central Asia, taking in the older states of the ancient Near East. It became the largest empire in history up to that time.

Why it matters: The Achaemenid state set patterns of imperial administration that later empires copied.

Aristotle was a Greek philosopher and polymath who founded the Peripatetic school at the Lyceum in Athens. His writings span logic, biology, physics, ethics, politics and the arts. He also tutored the young Alexander of Macedon.

Why it matters: His works set the groundwork for later European and Islamic scholarship and for the development of modern science.

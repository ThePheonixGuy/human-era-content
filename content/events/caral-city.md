The Caral culture of coastal Peru developed between 3000 and 1800 BCE. The city of Caral was inhabited from about 2600 to 2000 BCE, covers more than 60 hectares and is the largest recorded site in the Andean region dating to before 2000 BCE. The date here is approximate.

The Delhi Sultanate began in 1206 in former Ghurid lands in India. Its first sultan was Qutb ud-Din Aibak, a former slave of Muhammad Ghori, who ruled from Lahore until 1210.

Why it matters: It ruled much of northern India for over three centuries.

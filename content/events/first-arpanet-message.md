ARPANET was the first wide-area packet-switched network with distributed control, set up by the US Department of Defense's Advanced Research Projects Agency. The first message between two of its computers was sent in October 1969. It was one of the first networks to use the TCP/IP protocols that became the technical basis of the Internet.

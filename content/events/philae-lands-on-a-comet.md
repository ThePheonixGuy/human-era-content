The European Space Agency's Philae lander touched down on comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko on 12 November 2014, after a ten-year trip with the Rosetta spacecraft. It bounced when its anchoring harpoons failed to fire. It still sent back data before its batteries ran out.

The Umayyad caliph Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan commissioned the Dome of the Rock, which was most likely completed in 691 or 692 according to a Kufic inscription inside the dome. An alternative reading takes the inscription date as the start of construction.

Why it matters: A Kufic dedicatory inscription inside the dome supplies its date.

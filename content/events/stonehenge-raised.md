Stonehenge is a prehistoric megalithic monument on Salisbury Plain in Wiltshire, England. It has an outer ring of upright sarsen stones about 4 metres high and weighing about 25 tons, topped by lintels fixed with mortise and tenon joints. The sarsen circle dates to about 2500 BCE.

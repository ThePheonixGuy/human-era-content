On 26 April 1986 reactor 4 of the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant near Pripyat, then in the Soviet Union, exploded. The accident caused dozens of direct deaths and released radioactive material that led to the Chernobyl exclusion zone. More than 500,000 people took part in the response.

The Genpei War ran from the Battle of Uji on 20 June 1180 to the Battle of Dan-no-ura on 25 April 1185. The Minamoto clan won, and the Taira were destroyed.

Why it matters: Minamoto no Yoritomo later founded the Kamakura shogunate.

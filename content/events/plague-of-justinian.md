The plague of Justinian was a plague epidemic that struck the Mediterranean basin, Europe and the Near East, especially the Sasanian and Byzantine empires. It began in 541 and, according to the historian Procopius, the emperor Justinian I caught it and recovered in 542 at the height of the outbreak.

Why it matters: It killed a very large share of the population and weakened the Byzantine state.

The Holocene is the current geological epoch, beginning about 11,700 years ago, or around 9700 BCE, after the Last Glacial Period. It is an interglacial period within the ongoing Quaternary glacial cycles.

Why it matters: Its stable, milder climate made farming and permanent settlement possible.

Between 1519 and 1521 the Spanish conquistador Hernan Cortes, his small army and a large force of Indigenous allies fought the Aztec Triple Alliance. Tenochtitlan fell in 1521. Disease epidemics also weakened the Aztecs.

Why it matters: The conquest began Spanish colonial rule on the American mainland.

The Encyclopédie, edited by Denis Diderot and, until 1759, Jean le Rond d'Alembert, began publication in France in 1751 and was completed in 1772. It gathered articles on the sciences, arts and trades from many contributors known as the Encyclopédistes. It became a landmark of the Enlightenment.

At Tell Qaramel in northern Syria, excavators found circular stone towers dated between about 10,650 and 9,650 BCE. The timeline starts at 10,000 BCE, so the event sits at its edge.

Why it matters: The towers predate Göbekli Tepe and the tower at Jericho, which makes them the oldest known structures of their kind.

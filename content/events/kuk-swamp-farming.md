At Kuk Swamp in the highlands of Papua New Guinea, drainage ditches dating back about 9,000 years show early agricultural work, and taro was grown there. Evidence for bananas and sugar cane begins roughly 6,900 to 6,400 years ago. UNESCO recognised the site in 2008 as one of the places where people developed agriculture independently. The date here is approximate.

The Archaic stage of the Americas is dated from around 8000 BCE to 1000 BCE. People lived on nuts, seeds and shellfish, and the stage ends where settled farming is adopted, a date that differs from region to region.

Why it matters: The term gives archaeologists a shared frame for the long span between the first Americans and the first farming villages.

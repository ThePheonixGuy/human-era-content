The bronzes of Igbo-Ukwu in southeastern Nigeria are dated by radiocarbon to about 850 CE. They make Igbo-Ukwu the earliest known example of bronze casting in the region.

Why it matters: They show a highly skilled metalworking tradition in West Africa.

Between 1492 and 1504 the Italian navigator Christopher Columbus led four transatlantic voyages to the Americas on behalf of the Catholic Monarchs of Spain. The first landfall in the Caribbean came in October 1492. The voyages began the colonisation of the Americas.

Why it matters: They opened lasting contact between the hemispheres, with devastating consequences for Indigenous peoples.

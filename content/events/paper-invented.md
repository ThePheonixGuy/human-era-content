Court official Cai Lun of the Eastern Han dynasty presented an improved paper-making process to the emperor in 105 CE. He added tree bark and hemp scraps to the pulp, enabling mass production. Paper replaced bamboo strips and silk as a writing surface and later spread worldwide.

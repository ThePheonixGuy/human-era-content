King Ur-Nammu of the Third Dynasty of Ur built a massive stepped temple platform in his city, near present-day Nasiriyah. Centuries later, in the sixth century BCE, the Neo-Babylonian king Nabonidus restored it. Its remains still stand in southern Iraq.

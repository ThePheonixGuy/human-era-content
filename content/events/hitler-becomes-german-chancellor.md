Adolf Hitler, an Austrian-born politician and leader of the Nazi Party, became chancellor of Germany on 30 January 1933. In 1934 he took the title Fuhrer und Reichskanzler. Germany's invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939 under his leadership began the Second World War.

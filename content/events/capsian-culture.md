The Capsian was an Epipalaeolithic tradition of Algeria and Tunisia, dated from about 9000 to 5400 BCE in calibrated years. It is named after the town of Gafsa in Tunisia.

Why it matters: It is the long-running hunter-gatherer tradition of the Maghreb before farming arrived.

The Panama Canal opened to ships on 15 August 1914. The 82-kilometre waterway cuts across the Isthmus of Panama and joins the Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Locks lift ships up to Gatun Lake, an artificial lake, and ships no longer have to sail around South America.

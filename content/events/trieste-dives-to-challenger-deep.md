The bathyscaphe Trieste took Jacques Piccard and Don Walsh to the bottom of the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench on 23 January 1960. It was the first crewed vessel to reach the deepest known point in the ocean. The Swiss-designed vessel had been built in Italy.

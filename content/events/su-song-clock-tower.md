The water-powered astronomical clock tower of Su Song was completed in Kaifeng in 1094 under Emperor Zhezong, after a pilot model in 1088. It had an armillary sphere, a celestial globe and mannequins announcing the hours, and an escapement.

Why it matters: It was a landmark of mechanical timekeeping.

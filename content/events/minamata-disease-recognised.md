Minamata disease is a neurological illness caused by severe mercury poisoning. It was officially recognised in Japan in May 1956 in the city of Minamata, where a chemical factory had released mercury waste into the bay. Fish eaten by local families carried the poison, and it caused damage to the nerves and, in babies, to the brain.

The Dadiwan culture of eastern Gansu and Shaanxi, China, dates to about 5800 to 5400 BCE. Isotope analysis of dog remains suggests the people fed their dogs millet through the year, which counts as some of the earliest evidence of agriculture in East Asia.

Why it matters: It shows millet farming in the upper Wei valley before the Yangshao culture.

The Minoan civilisation was a Bronze Age culture centred on the island of Crete and is often called the first civilisation in Europe. It is known for monumental architecture, such as the palace at Knossos, and for its art. The Minoans traded widely, exporting farm products and luxury crafts in return for metals.

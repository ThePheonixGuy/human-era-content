The Scramble for Africa was the invasion, conquest and colonisation of most of Africa by seven Western European powers in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The powers were Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Spain and the United Kingdom, driven by the Second Industrial Revolution and the era of New Imperialism.

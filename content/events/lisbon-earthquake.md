The Great Lisbon earthquake struck the Iberian Peninsula and Northwest Africa at about 09:40 on Saturday 1 November 1755. Together with later fires and a tsunami it almost completely destroyed Lisbon and nearby areas. Seismologists estimate its magnitude at 7.7 or higher.

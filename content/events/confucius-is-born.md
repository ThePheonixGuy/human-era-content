Confucius, born Kong Qiu in 551 BCE, was a Chinese philosopher of the Spring and Autumn period. His teachings stressed personal and governmental morality, harmonious social relationships, righteousness and sincerity.

Why it matters: Confucianism shaped the culture of East Asia and the Sinosphere for more than two thousand years.

In 79 CE Mount Vesuvius in Campania, Italy, erupted in one of the deadliest eruptions in history. It threw a cloud of superheated gas and ash 33 kilometres high and buried the towns of Pompeii and Herculaneum. The preserved ruins show daily Roman life.

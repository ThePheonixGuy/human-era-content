Neolithic islanders on Gozo raised the Ġgantija megalithic temples from about 3600 BCE, with the larger southern temple dating to that time. The complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why it matters: It is considered the world's second-oldest surviving manmade religious structure after Göbekli Tepe, and it predates the Egyptian pyramids.

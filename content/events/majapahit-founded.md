Raden Wijaya was crowned on 10 November 1293 as the first king of Majapahit, after the Mongol invasion of Java, with his capital near Trowulan. The realm peaked from about 1350 to 1389 under Hayam Wuruk and the prime minister Gajah Mada.

Why it matters: It was the largest Indonesian-based empire of its age.

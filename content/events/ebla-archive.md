The royal archive of Ebla in Syria is dated to 2500 to 2350 BCE and holds about 20,000 cuneiform tablets, written in Sumerian and Eblaite. Most record economic matters, with royal letters and diplomatic documents among them.

Why it matters: The tablets reveal the politics and customs of the Levant in the mid-third millennium BCE and improved understanding of the Sumerian language.

The Song dynasty ruled China from 960 to 1279. Emperor Taizu founded it after seizing the throne of the Later Zhou and conquering the rest of the Ten Kingdoms. The Song often fought the Liao, Western Xia and Jin dynasties in northern China, and after losing the north it continued in the south.

Johannes Gutenberg was a German inventor and craftsman who created the movable-type printing press in Mainz around 1440. Movable type already existed in East Asia, but his press printed much faster.

Why it matters: Printing spread across the world and led to an information revolution, with mass circulation of books in Europe.

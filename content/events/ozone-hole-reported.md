Ozone depletion has been observed since the late 1970s as a lower total amount of stratospheric ozone and a much larger springtime decrease over the polar regions. British Antarctic Survey scientists reported the Antarctic ozone hole in 1985. The Montreal Protocol of 1987 later restricted the chemicals responsible.

Captain James Cook of the Royal Navy led three Pacific voyages between 1768 and 1779. In April 1770 his first voyage made the first recorded European visit to the east coast of Australia, landing at Botany Bay. He also completed the first recorded circumnavigation of the main islands of New Zealand.

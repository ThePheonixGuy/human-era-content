The Olmecs of Mexico's Gulf Coast, in Veracruz and Tabasco, flourished from about 1200 to 400 BCE and are often called one of the cradles of Mesoamerican civilisation. They carved colossal stone heads and built early ceremonial centres. They influenced later cultures such as the Maya.

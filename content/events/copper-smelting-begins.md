Early metalworkers in the Balkans and the Near East learned to smelt copper from its ore around the fifth millennium BCE. Copper tools and ornaments began to replace some stone ones, opening a period called the Chalcolithic or Copper Age. It prepared the way for bronze and, later, iron.

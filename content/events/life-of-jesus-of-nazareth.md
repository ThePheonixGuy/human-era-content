Jesus of Nazareth was a Jewish preacher and religious leader active in the Roman province of Judaea in the 1st century. He is the central figure of Christianity, the world's largest religion. He was probably born between about 6 and 4 BCE, and accounts of his life are in the New Testament gospels.

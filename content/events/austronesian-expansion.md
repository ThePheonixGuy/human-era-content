The Austronesian expansion was a large-scale migration of Austronesians from Taiwan, occurring around 3000 to 1500 BCE. With catamarans, outrigger boats, lashed-lug boats and the crab claw sail, they dispersed rapidly into the islands of the Indo-Pacific. The date here is approximate.

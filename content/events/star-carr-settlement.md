Star Carr is a Mesolithic site in North Yorkshire, England, first occupied between about 9335 and 9275 BCE and used for around 800 years. It has produced Britain's oldest known structure, 21 red deer stag skull-caps that may have been headdresses, and nearly 200 antler points. The date here is approximate.

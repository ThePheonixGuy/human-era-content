The Great Pyramid of Giza is the largest Egyptian pyramid and the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, and the only one still largely intact. It served as the tomb of the Fourth Dynasty pharaoh Khufu and was built around 2600 BCE over about 26 years.

Why it matters: It shows the labour and organisation of Old Kingdom Egypt.

Roman tradition dated the founding of the city by Romulus to 753 BCE. Archaeology shows that Rome grew from a union of hilltop villages in the early Iron Age, with settlement on the Palatine Hill and nearby heights. The traditional date was fixed centuries later by Roman writers.

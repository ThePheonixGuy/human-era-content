The Ancestral Puebloans began building the cliff dwellings of Mesa Verde toward the end of the 12th century and kept building through the 13th. The last inhabitants left around 1285, during a drought from 1276 to 1299.

Why it matters: The drought ended about seven centuries of continuous occupation of the mesa.

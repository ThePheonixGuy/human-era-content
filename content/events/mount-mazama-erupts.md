The climactic eruption of Mount Mazama in Oregon is dated to about 7,700 years ago by tree rings, or 6,845 radiocarbon years ago. Ash fell for about three years, and the collapsed volcano later became Crater Lake.

Why it matters: The collapsed volcano left the caldera that holds Crater Lake.

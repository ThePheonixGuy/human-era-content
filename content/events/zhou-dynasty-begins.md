The Zhou dynasty existed for 790 years, from about 1046 BCE until 256 BCE, the longest span of any dynasty in Chinese history. During the Western Zhou the royal house, surnamed Ji, held military control of the Wei River valley and North China Plain. In the Eastern Zhou, from 771 BCE, its authority became increasingly ceremonial.

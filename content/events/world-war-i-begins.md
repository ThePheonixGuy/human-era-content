Archduke Franz Ferdinand, heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne, and his wife Sophie were shot dead by the Bosnian Serb student Gavrilo Princip in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914. The assassination was one of the key events that led to World War I.

Why it matters: Alliance systems turned a regional crisis into a world war that killed millions.

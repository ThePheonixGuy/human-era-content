kind: "intro"
order: 3
focusHE: 501
spanYears: 1600
---
The count starts close to when the last ice age gave way to the warm Holocene, and around when people began farming. Cities, writing and every empire came after. Year 1 HE is 10,000 BCE, a round number near the start of it all.

The Vietnam War was an armed conflict in Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia between North Vietnam and South Vietnam and their allies. The Soviet Union and China supported the north, and the United States and other anti-communist nations supported the south. It was the second of the Indochina wars and a proxy war of the Cold War, ending in 1975.

On 3 September 1260 a Mamluk army from Egypt under Qutuz and Baybars defeated a Mongol force under Kitbuqa near Ain Jalut in the Jezreel Valley. The Mongols withdrew from the Levant.

Why it matters: It was the first major halt to Mongol advances.

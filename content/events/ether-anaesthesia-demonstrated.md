The dentist William T. G. Morton publicly demonstrated inhaled ether as a surgical anaesthetic at Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston on 16 October 1846. A tumour was removed from a patient who felt no pain. News of it spread quickly and the medical world accepted surgical anaesthesia.

Cuneiform is a logo-syllabic writing system, marked by wedge-shaped impressions, used to write several languages of the ancient Near East from the early Bronze Age until the 1st century BCE. It is the earliest known writing system and was first developed to write Sumerian in southern Mesopotamia.

Why it matters: Writing let information outlive its author and made record-keeping, law and literature possible.

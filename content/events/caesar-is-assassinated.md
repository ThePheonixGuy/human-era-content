Julius Caesar was assassinated on the Ides of March, 15 March 44 BCE, during a Senate session in the Curia of Pompey in Rome. A group of senators led by Marcus Junius Brutus, Gaius Cassius and Decimus Brutus stabbed him about 23 times. They justified it as a defence of the Republic.

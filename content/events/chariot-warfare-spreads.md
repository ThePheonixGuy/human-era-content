Light spoked-wheel chariots drawn by horses transformed warfare across the Near East. The oldest known chariots come from Sintashta burials in the Russian steppe around 1900 BCE, and the design spread to Egypt, Anatolia and beyond. Armies built around them dominated for several centuries.

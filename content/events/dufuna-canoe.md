The Dufuna canoe, found in 1987 near the village of Dufuna in Yobe State, Nigeria, is a dugout 8.4 metres long. Radiocarbon dating of charcoal near the find puts it at 8,500 to 8,000 years old, and Wikipedia describes it as the world's second-oldest known boat. The site is linked to the old shoreline of Lake Chad. The date here is approximate.

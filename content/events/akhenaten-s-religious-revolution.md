Akhenaten, born Amenhotep IV, ruled Egypt from about 1353 to 1336 BCE as the tenth king of the Eighteenth Dynasty. In his fifth year he took his new name and promoted the sun disk Aten above the traditional gods. His religious reform faded after his reign.

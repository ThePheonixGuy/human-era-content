The Great Depression was a severe worldwide economic downturn from 1929 to 1939. It began in the United States, where the Wall Street crash of 1929 is often taken as its start, and brought mass unemployment, falling trade and waves of bank failures.

Why it matters: It reshaped economic policy and fed the political extremism of the 1930s.

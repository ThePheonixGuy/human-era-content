In January 1922 the first patient, a 14-year-old boy with diabetes, was treated with an extract of insulin in Toronto. Frederick Banting and Charles Best had isolated it, working with John Macleod and James Collip. Banting and Macleod won the 1923 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

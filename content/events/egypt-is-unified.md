Narmer was an ancient Egyptian king whose reign began at the end of the 4th millennium BCE. Many scholars regard him as the unifier of Egypt and founder of the First Dynasty. The date is approximate and the unification was probably a gradual process.

Why it matters: The unified pharaonic state that followed lasted for nearly three thousand years.

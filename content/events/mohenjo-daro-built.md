Mohenjo-daro was built around 2500 BCE and held about 40,000 people at its height, making it one of the largest cities of the Indus Valley Civilisation. It had a planned grid of rectilinear buildings and the Great Bath, a brick pool lined with bitumen.

Why it matters: It shows urban planning and public sanitation at a scale unmatched in its era.

World War I, also called the Great War, was fought from 1914 to 1918 between the Allies and the Central Powers. It was one of the deadliest conflicts in history, with 15 to 22 million deaths, and it brought new weapons such as tanks, aircraft and poison gas into use.

Why it matters: Four empires collapsed in its wake, and the peace that followed set the stage for World War II.

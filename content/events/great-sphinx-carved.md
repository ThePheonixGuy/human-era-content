A limestone outcrop at Giza was carved into a lion with a human head during the reign of Khafre, around 2550 BCE. It is the oldest known monumental sculpture in Egypt and faces east across the Nile. It is part of the Memphite Necropolis and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

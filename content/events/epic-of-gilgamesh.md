The Epic of Gilgamesh is an epic from ancient Mesopotamia and the oldest surviving example of narrative literature. It began as five Sumerian poems about Gilgamesh, king of Uruk, some possibly from the Third Dynasty of Ur. Later writers combined them into a single Akkadian epic.

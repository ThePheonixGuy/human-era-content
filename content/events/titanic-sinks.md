The liner RMS Titanic struck an iceberg on her maiden voyage from Southampton to New York on the night of 14 April 1912 and sank in the early hours of the next day. About 1,500 of the 2,208 people aboard died. It led to new maritime safety rules, including lifeboat places for everyone on board.

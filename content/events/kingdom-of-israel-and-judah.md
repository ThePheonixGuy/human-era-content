The first extra-biblical mention of Israel is on the Merneptah Stele of 1208 BCE. According to the Hebrew Bible a United Monarchy under Saul, David and Solomon existed around 1000 BCE, though historians debate its extent. A Kingdom of Israel is documented in the Southern Levant in antiquity.

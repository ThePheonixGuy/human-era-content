The Axial Age is a term coined in 1949 by the German philosopher Karl Jaspers for broad changes in religious and philosophical thought that took place in several regions from about the 8th to the 3rd century BCE. It covers figures such as Confucius, the Buddha, the Hebrew prophets and the Greek philosophers.

Why it matters: Ideas about ethics and the self from this period still shape religious and philosophical traditions.

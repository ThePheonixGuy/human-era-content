King Narasingha Deva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty built the Konark Sun Temple around 1250. It is shaped as a stone chariot of the sun god Surya, with 24 carved wheels and seven horses.

Why it matters: Its 24 wheels correspond to the months of the Hindu calendar.

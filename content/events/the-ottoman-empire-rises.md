The Ottoman Empire was a state centred in modern Turkey that spanned much of Southeastern Europe, West Asia and North Africa from the 14th century to the early 20th century. Tradition dates its founding by Osman I to about 1299. It also controlled parts of southeastern Central Europe from the early 16th to the early 18th century.

Why it matters: It controlled the crossroads between Europe and Asia for six centuries.

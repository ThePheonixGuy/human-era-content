Yuri Gagarin, a Soviet pilot, became the first person in space. He was launched from the Baikonur Cosmodrome in Vostok 1 on 12 April 1961 and completed one orbit of Earth. The flight took 108 minutes.

Why it matters: It was a major Soviet success in the Space Race and opened the era of human spaceflight.

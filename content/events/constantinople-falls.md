The Ottoman Empire captured Constantinople, capital of the Byzantine Empire, on 29 May 1453 after a siege of 53 days that began on 6 April. The city under Sultan Mehmed II became the Ottoman capital.

Why it matters: Its fall ended the Byzantine Empire, which had lasted since Roman times.

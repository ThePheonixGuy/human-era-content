The American Revolution (1765 to 1789) began as a rebellion in Britain's Thirteen Colonies and became a revolution. Victory in the Revolutionary War (1775 to 1783) and British recognition in the Treaty of Paris created the United States.

Why it matters: It founded a republic on written ideas of rights and self-government.

In 909 Kutama Berber forces led by Abu Abdallah al-Shi'i overthrew the Aghlabids and took Raqqada in Tunisia. Abdallah al-Mahdi Billah became the first Fatimid caliph.

Why it matters: The Fatimids went on to conquer Egypt in 969 and found Cairo.

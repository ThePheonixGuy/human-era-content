In 929 Abd al-Rahman III proclaimed himself caliph, raising the Umayyad emirate of Córdoba to a caliphate. It lasted until 1031, when it fractured into independent taifa kingdoms.

Why it matters: It replaced the emirate that had ruled Córdoba since 756.

The Ninety-five Theses, or Disputation on the Power and Efficacy of Indulgences, were written in 1517 by Martin Luther, then a professor of moral theology at the University of Wittenberg. They are seen as having launched the Protestant Reformation.

Why it matters: The Reformation split Western Christianity and reshaped European politics and religion.

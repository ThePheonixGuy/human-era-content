The Chinese Buddhist monk Xuanzang began his pilgrimage to India in 629 CE and was back in China by 645. He brought home many Sanskrit texts and translated 75 distinct works, including some of the most important Mahayana scriptures.

Why it matters: His travels carried Buddhist texts and an eyewitness record of Central and South Asia into China.

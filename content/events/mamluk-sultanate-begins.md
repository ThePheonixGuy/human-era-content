The Mamluk Sultanate began in 1250 when the Ayyubid dynasty in Egypt fell, marked by the murder of Turanshah on 2 May. It lasted until the Ottoman conquest of Cairo in January 1517.

Why it matters: It ruled Egypt and the Levant for more than two and a half centuries.

The stone tower at Jericho was built around 8000 BCE in the Pre-Pottery Neolithic A period. Kathleen Kenyon showed in the 1950s that it was Neolithic, far older than the date of about 1400 BCE that John Garstang had proposed.

Why it matters: It is among the oldest monumental structures in the world.
